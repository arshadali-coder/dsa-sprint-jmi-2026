01 / Program DNA

What this is — and
what it is not

🎯

Target-Based Self-Study

Weekly problem targets, not lecture hours. Participants own their pace. Council provides the map and checkpoints.

🤝

Accountability Over Teaching

Not a university course. No heavy theory. Concepts are tools for solving problems — taught when needed, not upfront.

🔰

Beginner-Optimized

Single track, tuned for newcomers. Advanced students may mentor or fast-track, but program outcomes are measured at the beginner baseline.

⚡

Serious Commitment Model

4 hrs/day × 5 days = 20 hrs/week. Participants who sign up understand this is a sprint, not a stroll.

04 / Onboarding

Participant Qualification

STEP 01

Interest Form

Name, year, branch, prior coding experience, platform handles (LC/CF/GFG), availability confirmation (20 hrs/week).

STEP 02

Screening Test

30-min timed mini-test: 5 basic problems. Not a filter for skill — a filter for serious intent and baseline check.

STEP 03

Orientation Call

15-min group walkthrough of expectations, commitment, roadmap, tools, and mentor pod assignment.

STEP 04

Pod Assignment

Assigned to a mentor pod of 8–10 participants. Mentor is an advanced student or returning member.

05 / Operations

Operational Infrastructure

📣 Communication

  • Primary channel: WhatsApp or Discord server with #general, #doubts, #topic-week-N, #leaderboard, #memes
  • Doubt threads stay in #doubts — mentors respond within 24 hours on weekdays
  • Weekly topic drop pinned every Monday morning with a 1-page PDF guide + problem list
  • No cold DM culture — all questions public for collective benefit

📊 Progress Tracking

  • Google Sheet shared tracker: each participant self-logs problems solved daily
  • Honor system — enforced by Friday mini-contest performance cross-check
  • Pod mentor reviews their 8–10 students weekly and flags inactivity
  • Automated leaderboard update every Saturday (from tracker or LC API if feasible)

🏆 Weekly Contest

  • Every Friday, 60–90 min timed contest (Codeforces div-4 style or custom)
  • 3–4 problems from the week's topic — auto-graded where possible
  • Score contributes to leaderboard (20% weight) alongside total problems solved
  • Week 8: Final Grand Contest — cross-pod, longer format, with prizes

🔔 Check-ins

  • Each pod: 30-min weekly sync (async voice note acceptable for offline students)
  • Mentor fills a 5-field pod report: attendance, avg progress, blockers, standout, at-risk
  • Council runs one all-hands recap call mid-sprint (Week 4) and end-sprint (Week 8)
  • At-risk flag triggers a 1:1 DM from mentor, not a public callout

📚 Resources

  • Curated weekly PDF: topic summary (1 page), concept cheatsheet, 20–25 tagged problems
  • Problem list tagged: must-do / optional / bonus (hard)
  • No prescribed video lectures — but 2–3 vetted YouTube links per topic provided
  • Solutions posted Sunday evening for all must-do problems (no peeking before Friday)

⚠️ Drop & Strike Policy

  • Miss 1 week with no communication → mentor yellow-flag
  • Miss 2 consecutive weeks → removed from active leaderboard, certificate revoked
  • Re-entry allowed once per sprint with mentor approval
  • No shame, but slots are limited — inactive spots can be opened to waitlist
06 / Mentor Pods

Pod Structure

Pod Composition

8–10 beginners · 1 mentor

  • Mentor is an advanced student (100+ LC) or returning Coding Council member
  • Ideally same branch/year group for scheduling ease
  • Pods are fixed for the sprint — no reshuffling unless escalated
  • Pod names optional but fun (Pod Alpha, Pod Zeta, etc.)

Mentor Responsibilities

~3–5 hrs/week commitment

  • Respond to doubt questions within 24 hours on weekdays
  • Weekly 30-min pod sync (can be async)
  • Flag at-risk participants to council leads
  • Weekly 5-field pod report (5 min effort)
  • Optional: host a 1-hr explanation session on the week's hardest concept

Mentor Incentives

Why mentors should want to do this

  • Certificate of Mentorship from Coding Council
  • Teaching is one of the best ways to solidify your own DSA knowledge
  • Priority in future council leadership / event roles
  • Resume line item — "Pod Mentor, CC DSA Sprint @ JMI" is real experience
  • Peer recognition and social standing during the sprint

09 / Pending Decisions

Still to Finalize

🔢 Cohort Size

  • Recommended: 30–60 participants (3–6 pods of 10) for first run
  • Too small: leaderboard loses energy; too large: mentor quality drops
  • Hard cap at 80 without additional council bandwidth

📅 Sprint Dates

  • Confirm JMI summer calendar for exam-free 8-week window
  • Avoid first 2 weeks of summer (people still settling) and last week (pre-term prep)
  • Monday start strongly preferred for weekly rhythm

🛠 Tooling

  • Tracker: Google Sheets vs Notion vs custom (start with Sheets)
  • Contests: Codeforces mashup, LeetCode weekly, or self-hosted on HackerRank
  • Communication: WhatsApp (low friction) vs Discord (better threading)

🎁 Prizes

  • Sprint Champions: council merch, public recognition, priority at future events
  • Weekly winner: small callout + leaderboard badge
  • Budget depends on council funds — even zero-budget works with recognition alone
